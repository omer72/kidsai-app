### Maker's first comment

**Post this immediately after the launch goes live** — first comments are critical for ranking and they're how you start the conversation that pulls in upvotes.

```
Hey PH 👋 I'm Omer, the maker of Kidsit AI.

I built this for the moments parenting tries you the most. The
kind where your kid is on the floor at 6 PM, screaming about a
book you said no to, and you've been a parent for years and you
still don't know what to do — and you sure as hell can't search
"how to handle bedtime tantrum age 4" while it's happening.

Here's how it works: you press and hold a mic, you whisper what
just happened — exactly the way you'd tell a friend on the phone.
Ten seconds later you get back four things:

• What happened — a clear, judgment-free recap
• Why it happened — the developmental + emotional read
• What to try next time — three specific actions, including the
  exact sentence to say to your kid
• Tonight, before bed — one warm thing to say at bedtime to reconnect

Per-kid memory means each child gets their own profile. The more
moments you log, the smarter the guidance gets for your kid
specifically. When you mark something as "didn't help," the next
answer tries a different developmental angle instead of repeating
advice that didn't work.

Three things I want to be honest about:

1. It's iOS only (for now)
2. The guidance is grounded in Adlerian / Haim Ginott / Faber-Mazlish
   parenting research — empathy first, advice last. It's NOT therapy
   or medical advice.
3. Your kid data stays on your device. We don't sell it. We don't
   train models on it.

Free to use with 1 kid. Premium unlocks multiple kids + pattern
detection over time. 7-day free trial on both plans.

For parents reading this — what's the 6 PM moment of your day you
wish you had this for? Tell me and I'll respond to everyone.
```
